AI agent development & human-controlled autonomy

Expotech develops advanced AI agents and agentic systems designed to automate complex business processes, coordinate workflows, analyze information, and take intelligent action across enterprise environments.

Our development methodology incorporates AIM™, the Agha Implementation Model, developed by Dr. Raed (Rod) Ghani Agha: a structured framework for defining an AI agent's authority, boundaries, risk, oversight and accountability throughout its lifecycle. The framework is documented in his book The Last Command and operationalized through the AIM Command Center™.
