Controlled autonomy

The question changes from "what can the AI do?"

to "what should the AI be allowed to do?"

Expotech approaches AI agent development with human authority at the center of the architecture. Using AIM™, the Agha Implementation Model, organizations define an agent's permitted purpose, data access, actions, financial authority, operating time, delegation authority, system access, and consequence boundaries before autonomous capabilities are expanded.
