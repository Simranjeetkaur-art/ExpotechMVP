The Last Command

Building Powerful AI Without Surrendering Human Control

By Dr. Raed (Rod) Ghani Agha

The book examines the transition from AI as a tool to AI as an autonomous actor within organizations. It introduces AIM™, the Agha Implementation Model, and explores the architecture, governance principles, authority boundaries, risk models, organizational controls and leadership practices required to deploy powerful AI without unintentionally transferring control from people to machines.

It asks one of the defining questions of the autonomous-AI era: when machines can act, who remains in command?
