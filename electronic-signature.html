How it works

Tag. Sign. Secure and file.

No monthly fees or per-signature charges. One quick installation lets you secure and tag any document, send it for signatures, notarize it and file it electronically.

01

Tag

Open any PDF in the tagging software, add the signers, and drag and drop signature, notary seal or court stamp tags where they're needed. The document is saved in a secured format whose contents can't be changed.

02

Sign

Email it to the signers. They download the free signing software and sign with an epad, epen or mouse; the software guides them through the document.

03

Secure & file

Signatures, seals and the document are secured together, encrypted and filed electronically. The physical paper is eliminated.
